💡 The idea — what we tried to break

The frozen decoder shipped at P5 with a band-C-shaped risk: one systematic weakness (dead-ball mis-calibration), no structural bug. P6 asks whether external-only evidence can move it: does realistic (phase-correlated) missingness move the plateau (E2), do challenge-faithful labels change what "good" means (E1), does grid closure (E3) or a fill re-check (E11) hide a gain, and does restart timing sharpen D (E7)? Plus the compliance loop: the FAQ says 2,934 pre-kickoff frames are all D and we emit 37 % — fix it generically or document why not (E10).

Why it could move spellF1: under uniform masking 38–48 % of hand-overs have no ball on the boundary frame; if real missingness concentrates there, the switch gate reverts true switches and the decoder under-segments (up to ±0.03). Every hypothesis below was tested against the same bar: > 0.02 paired on IDSSE, ≥ 5/7 matches, no-harm on the other tables.
