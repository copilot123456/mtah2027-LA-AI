💡 The idea — what we tried to break

Parallel work on one frozen pipeline usually means everyone edits the same decoder, fill and config files — and Wednesday's merge becomes conflict resolution. The "break" here is structural: make the pipeline a LEGO instruction sheet. Each stage becomes a brick with a fixed shape (what it requires, what it provides); the declaration file is the sheet. Swapping a brick never restructures the model, and the audited core stays untouched on main.

Why it could help us win: it converts team throughput into score attempts instead of merge conflicts — every member's idea reaches the Wednesday sync as a one-line diff plus evidence, ready to compare under P6 as the shared KPI.
