🖥️ One-screen summary

What Jose built

A clean adapter that runs his Kaggle-trained ball detector (RT-DETR-L on 320 px tiles) and a small "FoT v2" transformer over the match video, and plugs the result into our pipeline as an alternative ball stage. Well structured, honest about its gaps, 31 tests.

The honest verdict

Good engineering, not usable for the submission as it stands. It only processes one 30-second clip (under 0.5 % of the graded frames), the way its evidence enters the pipeline is upside-down, and — the real blocker — nobody can say what data the detector was trained on or where its ~45 thresholds came from.

What I did

Fixed every defect that could be fixed without changing results (proven identical against Jose's original with the real models), added switches to measure the rest, and built a training/ toolkit: external-data guard, tile builder, recovered training recipe, honest evaluation, FoT training, plus the RTX SOP.

What happens next

The branch is on GitHub; the RTX agent runs SOP phases 1–6b (setup, GPU checks, full-HD equivalence) with no dataset needed. Real training starts at phase 7 — and needs Jose's answer on the dataset (see the questions below).

Verdict: ITERATE. Keep the work, do not adopt it into the submission. Confidence: high on the code findings (each re-checked by an independent skeptic, most reproduced by running code); the GPU side is untested because this laptop has no GPU.

Is it good? Three honest lenses

Code quality: yes. Every fix is tested and provably output-neutral; the training tools were reviewed adversarially and the problems found in them fixed too.
Usefulness for the 17 Nov submission: low. Even a perfect detector would not change the file without a full-match pass, the evidence fix and a port onto the adopted pipeline — and the data question blocks adoption anyway.
Usefulness for a better detector: high. The recipe was recovered from the checkpoint itself, every experiment is one declared change, and "better" is now measured the way the pipeline uses the detector.

🚀 The RTX plan (SOP v1.1)

  • Phases 1–4 · probe, code, environment, preflightno data needed
    Read-only machine probe (GPU, driver, memory variant 8 or 16 GB, sudo), clone/switch branch, pinned venv (torch 2.15 + ultralytics 8.4.212), preflight and tests. Driver install or reboot = stop and ask you.
  • Phases 5–6b · prove it on the GPUhanded over from this laptop
    Synthetic training smoke run, real-model acceptance test, and the full-HD check that the reviewed module equals Jose's original. First real throughput number.
  • Phase 7 · dataset gatestops for you
    Needs the external dataset with name, licence and source — or Jose's prepared tiles (then the committed checkpoint must re-score ≈0.65 mAP50 on them as an integrity check).
  • Phases 8–11 · measure, reproduce, evaluate
    Time one probe epoch before planning; retrain the recovered recipe; compare with Jose's checkpoint on a held-out game — must come out "no evidence of a difference".
  • Phase 12 · experiments, one change each
    2× up-sampled tiles (the literature's strongest lever for tiny balls), no bias-warm-up spike, hard negatives, no mosaic, longer schedule, more games. Only a "B better" verdict on a held-out game counts.
  • Phases 13–14 · FoT (optional) and report
    FoT training is a documented reconstruction; the agent writes its own report, commits locally and asks you before pushing.

❓ Questions for Jose

Jose — great work on the adapter. To use it in the submission, and to retrain it on the RTX PC exactly as you did, we need the answers below. The first three are the most important: the technical report has to name the training data, and the rules say anything trained or tuned must use external data only. Short answers are fine; links or files are even better.

#QuestionWhy we ask
1Training data. Which dataset do games 4, 6 and 9 come from? Exact name and link (Kaggle slug, Hugging Face repo or other), its licence or terms of use, and whether it was a public dataset attached to the notebook or a private upload. How many games does it have, and what are the other folds?Must be named and licensed in the technical report; needed to rebuild the data on the RTX PC.
2Thresholds. On which footage were the constants at the top of src/fot_video.py chosen — the confidence floors (0.07, 0.18), the profile thresholds (0.30 / 0.40 and FoT 0.45 / 0.72), the 0.68 and 0.72 cut-offs, the gate radius and speeds, the smoothing noise? Was any value changed after watching clips of our match (BAR-ATH)?Tuning must use external data only; if a value came from our match, it has to be re-derived on external games.
3Where it ran. Did the notebook run locally or on Kaggle / another cloud service, and was our match video or homography ever uploaded there?The rules forbid uploading the provided data to outside services; we need to state this correctly in the report.
4The notebook(s). Can you share the Kaggle notebook(s) — at least the tile-building code and the FoT v2 training loop — its "Input" list, and if possible the prepared tile set (tmp_fold0_tiles)? Which Python, torch and CUDA versions did it use?Exact reproduction instead of our reconstruction; with your tiles the RTX agent can check the copy re-scores your recorded mAP50 of 0.65.
5Tiles. Were the 320 px tiles cut on a grid or centred on the ball? How many background tiles per ball tile? Were non-pitch pixels masked out (as at inference)? What box size did you use for the ball label? Does frame_step 2 mean every second frame of a 25 fps sequence?Our tile builder had to assume these; a mismatch between training and inference tiles costs accuracy.
6Labels. Where do the ball positions come from: hand-drawn boxes, interpolated tracks, projected tracking data, or another detector? Are occluded balls labelled? What camera, resolution and frame rate are the source videos?Tells us how far the training footage is from our tactical camera and how much to trust the labels.
7FoT v2 training. What exactly is hit5 (ball within 5 px of the prediction?), which losses did you use and how were they weighted, which detector and thresholds produced its training inputs, and what does best_fot_loss 1.022 measure?Our FoT trainer is a reconstruction based on five assumptions; your answer replaces them.
8Smaller ones. Was SoccerNet "H250" (mentioned in your earlier notes) used in this training? What does "FoT" stand for?Completes the provenance record.
